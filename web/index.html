                          Why only 2 MB?
                          
          
        
                          
        
                          
          Slynk is built for the small things that matter most. Your data is split into small pieces and passed through a
          worldwide network of computers, which mix it with everyone else’s traffic. That keeps it private, but it’s slow:
          2 MB takes about four minutes.
        
                          
        
                          To make the most of the space, Slynk compresses what it can first, and can make photos, videos and documents smaller.
                          
        
                          More on the About page
                          
        
                          
          
        
                          
      
                          
                              

    
    
    
      
                          
        
                          
          
                          Run Slynk on your computer
                          
          
        
                          
        
                          
          You can download Slynk and run it on your own computer: Windows, macOS or Linux, not phones. It then makes no
          requests to slynk.me’s website, so there are no visits for it to see, and the code you run stays the same until
          you replace it.
        
                          
        
                          
          It still needs the internet: it sends and receives through the xx network’s mixnet, and uses slynk.me’s key
          server for each link’s key, so it can’t work offline.
        
                          
        
                          
          See the downloads
          
        
                          
      
                          
                              
    

    
    
      
                          
        
                          
          
                          What’s happening?
                          
          
        
                          
        
                          
          Slynk is making your files smaller, right here in your browser, so they send faster and everything you’ve chosen
          fits in one link.
          Nothing has been sent: your files haven’t left this device, and your originals stay untouched.
        
                          
        
                          
          When it has finished and you click the button to send your files, they will be sent across the mixnet, in small
          pieces mixed with everyone else’s traffic. That keeps them private, but it’s slow, so each link carries 2 MB at
          most. Making photos, videos and documents smaller lets as much as you want to send fit through.
