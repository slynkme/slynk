-- BIP-39 word lists --
Slynk's suggested passwords use the BIP-39 English word list (some of its
words left out, web/src/ui/words.ts), and an inbox's 12 words are BIP-39 words
(the mnemonic code in xx's crypto, with go-bip39, in the WebAssembly). BIP-39
and its word lists are by Marek Palatinus, Pavol Rusnak, Aaron Voisine and
Sean Bowe, under the MIT License:
https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0039.mediawiki

-- Diceware word lists, in go-diceware (in the WebAssembly, linked by xx's crypto) --
The EFF's word lists for random passphrases (2016), by the Electronic Frontier
Foundation, under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). From
https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2016/07/new-wordlists-random-passphrases
The English Diceware word list by Arnold G. Reinhold, who licenses his rights
to it under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). From
https://theworld.com/~reinhold/diceware.html . Diceware is a trademark of
A G Reinhold.
